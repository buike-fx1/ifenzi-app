Hair & Beauty Tips

Simple advice from our stylists to keep your look fresh between visits.

Wigs
5 Wig Care Tips to Make Your Install Last
Read more

Brush gently from the ends upward, never from the roots down, to avoid tangles. Wash your wig only when needed with a mild shampoo and cool water, then condition the lengths. Let it air dry on a wig stand instead of using high heat. Wrap your hair and wig in a satin bonnet or scarf at night to reduce friction. Finally, book a refresh with your stylist every few weeks so the install stays neat and secure.

Braids
How to Keep Your Braids Looking Fresh for Longer
Read more

Protect your braids at night with a satin bonnet or scarf. Moisturise your scalp lightly with a non-greasy oil or leave-in spray instead of heavy creams, which cause build-up. Avoid pulling the braids into very tight styles, and cleanse the scalp gently with a diluted shampoo every two weeks. Most braided styles look best for about four to six weeks, so plan your take-down before frizz and new growth become too much.

Hair Care
Why Regular Hair and Scalp Treatments Matter
Read more

A healthy scalp is the foundation of healthy hair. Deep conditioning restores moisture, while protein treatments help strengthen hair that is dry, colour-treated or heat-styled. A regular treatment every few weeks keeps hair soft, easier to style and less prone to breakage. Our stylists can recommend the right treatment for your hair type during your visit.
